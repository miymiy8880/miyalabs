WHAT IS NIACINAMIDE?

Vitamin B3 derivative used in cosmetic formulations.

Niacinamide, also known as nicotinamide, is a form of vitamin B3. In cosmetics, it is primarily classified as a skin and hair conditioning ingredient.

N B3

COSMETIC FUNCTION

What does it do?

01

Skin conditioning

CIR identifies niacinamide as functioning primarily as a skin-conditioning ingredient in cosmetics.

02

Hair conditioning

Niacinamide is also described as a hair-conditioning ingredient.

03

Formulation ingredient

It is incorporated into a range of cosmetic product types, including skincare and haircare formulations.

SAFETY

Cosmetic safety

The Cosmetic Ingredient Review Expert Panel concluded that niacinamide and niacin were safe as used in cosmetics in its published safety assessment.

The assessment reported clinical testing in which niacinamide was not a significant skin irritant, sensitizer, or photosensitizer at the concentrations evaluated.
