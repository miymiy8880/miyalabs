OVERVIEW

What is niacinamide?

Niacinamide, also known as nicotinamide, is a form of vitamin B3. It is widely used in cosmetic formulations and is commonly incorporated into skin-care products.

Its use in a formulation should be assessed in the context of the complete formulation, intended application, concentration, product format, and applicable regulatory requirements.
